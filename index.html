The six-week plan

Build a content system you can keep up with.

One live masterclass each week, followed by a practical step you can fit around your job.

WEEK 01

Direction & strategy

Stop guessing what your page is about.

  • Choose who you want to help and what you want to be known for.
  • Set content themes that make sense for your experience.

The goal: Know what your content is for and who it serves.

WEEK 02

Finding ideas

Never start with a blank page again.

  • Find topics in the questions people ask and the work you already do.
  • Collect angles in a simple idea bank and content planner.

The goal: A repeatable way to find your next post.

WEEK 03

Scripting & storytelling

Make people care about the point you’re making.

  • Turn an idea into a clear opening, story and takeaway.
  • Write a script that sounds like you, not a copied format.

The goal: A script you can actually film.

WEEK 04

Filming & visual storytelling

Use the time and gear you have.

  • Plan scenes, framing and b-roll before you press record.
  • Film around your working week, with faceless options too.

The goal: Footage ready to turn into a post.

WEEK 05

Editing

Finish the piece instead of polishing it forever.

  • Cut for clarity and pace, without getting lost in effects.
  • Use the Premiere Pro pack and sound effects where they help.

The goal: A finished edit you’re ready to publish.

WEEK 06

Repurposing & publishing

Keep going after the cohort ends.

  • Adapt one idea for short videos and carousels.
  • Set a realistic posting rhythm around your job.

The goal: A content routine you can keep using.

Questions

What if I work full-time?

The cohort is built to fit around work and real life, with one practical focus each week.

Do I need to show my face?

No. You can make face-to-camera, voiceover or faceless content.

Will I reach 10K followers in six weeks?

We can’t promise a follower number. The cohort gives you skills and a process to work towards your goals.

What tools do I need?

Start with the phone, camera and editing tools you already have. You do not need new gear to begin.
